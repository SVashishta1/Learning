# Repairs and servicing

A basic service costs 45 euros and includes adjusting the gears and brakes, checking the wheels and cleaning and oiling the chain. A full service costs 110 euros and also includes taking apart, cleaning and regreasing the bearings.

Every new bike comes with one free basic service, which should be done within the first 3 months, because new cables stretch.

Puncture repairs cost 12 euros per wheel while you wait, usually in about 20 minutes. Most other repairs are ready within 2 working days. In spring (March to May) it can take up to 5 working days, so book early.

You can book a repair online, by phone on 0123 456 789, or by walking into the shop.
