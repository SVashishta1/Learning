# Returns and refunds

Customers can return a new bike within 30 days of purchase for a full refund, as long as it has been ridden less than 50 kilometres and comes back with the receipt. After 30 days, or with more than 50 kilometres on it, we offer store credit only.

Accessories such as helmets, lights and locks can be returned within 14 days if they are unused and in their original packaging. Helmets that have been worn cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons.

Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 3 to 5 working days to appear. Cash purchases are refunded in cash on the same day.

Sale items marked "final sale" cannot be returned, but they are still covered by the warranty.
