# Opening hours and contact

Our shop on River Street is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00, and on Saturday from 10:00 to 16:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

The workshop closes 30 minutes before the shop, so the last repairs are accepted at 17:30 on weekdays.

You can email us at hello@riverside-bikes.example. We answer emails within one working day.

Test rides are free. Please bring a photo ID, which we keep while you ride.
