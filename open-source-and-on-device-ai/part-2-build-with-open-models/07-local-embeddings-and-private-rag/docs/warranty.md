# Warranty

Every bike frame we sell has a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects for the first owner. Forks and suspension parts are covered for 2 years. Wheels, brakes, gears and other components are covered for 1 year.

The warranty does not cover normal wear such as tyres, brake pads, chains and cassettes, or damage from crashes, racing or incorrect assembly by someone else.

To make a warranty claim, bring the bike and the receipt to any Riverside Bikes shop. We inspect the bike within 2 working days and tell you if the claim is accepted. Approved repairs are free, including labour.

Electric bike batteries are covered for 2 years or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first.
