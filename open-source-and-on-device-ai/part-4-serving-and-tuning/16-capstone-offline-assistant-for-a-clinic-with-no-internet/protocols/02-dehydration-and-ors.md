# Diarrhoea and dehydration, oral rehydration (Riverside Community Clinic, fictional protocol RCC-02)

> FICTIONAL TRAINING SAMPLE. Written for a programming course. NOT medical advice. Do not use for real patients.

## Recognising dehydration
- **Some dehydration:** restless or irritable, thirsty and drinks eagerly, sunken eyes, skin pinch goes back slowly.
- **Severe dehydration:** very sleepy or unconscious, not able to drink or drinks poorly, skin pinch goes back very slowly (more than 2 seconds). Severe dehydration is an emergency: start the referral at once (RCC-05).

## Preparing oral rehydration solution (ORS)
1. Wash your hands with soap.
2. Pour exactly 1 litre of clean, boiled and cooled water into the clinic's marked jug.
3. Empty one whole ORS sachet into the water and stir until it dissolves.
4. Label the jug with the time. Throw away any ORS left after 24 hours and make a new jug.

## Giving ORS
- Give small sips from a cup or spoon, every 1 to 2 minutes. For young children, use a spoon.
- If the child vomits, wait 10 minutes, then continue more slowly.
- Keep breastfeeding and continue normal food as soon as the child is hungry.
- Zinc tablets are given according to the clinic dosing chart for the child's age.

## Before the family goes home
Show the caregiver how to prepare ORS, give 2 sachets to take home, and explain the danger signs that mean they must return immediately.
