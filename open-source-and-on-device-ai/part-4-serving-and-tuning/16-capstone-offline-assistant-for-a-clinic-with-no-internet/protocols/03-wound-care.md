# Cleaning and dressing small wounds (Riverside Community Clinic, fictional protocol RCC-03)

> FICTIONAL TRAINING SAMPLE. Written for a programming course. NOT medical advice. Do not use for real patients.

## Which wounds this covers
Small cuts and grazes that are not bleeding heavily. Deep wounds, wounds with heavy bleeding that does not stop after 10 minutes of firm pressure, animal bites to the face, and wounds with something stuck in them must be referred (RCC-05).

## Steps
1. Wash your hands and put on clean gloves.
2. If it is bleeding, press firmly on the wound with clean gauze for 10 minutes without lifting.
3. Rinse the wound with plenty of clean running water or sterile saline for at least 5 minutes to wash out dirt.
4. Gently remove remaining dirt with sterile gauze. Do not scrub.
5. Cover with a sterile non-stick dressing and a bandage.
6. Write the wound location, size and the time in the visit book.

## Tetanus
Ask about tetanus vaccination. If the person is not sure they are up to date, or the wound is dirty, follow the vaccination register procedure and tell the nurse in charge.

## Follow-up
Ask the patient to return in 2 days to change the dressing, or earlier if there is spreading redness, swelling, pus, a bad smell, red lines going up the limb, or fever. These are signs of infection.
