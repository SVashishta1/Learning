# Danger signs and referral to the district hospital (Riverside Community Clinic, fictional protocol RCC-05)

> FICTIONAL TRAINING SAMPLE. Written for a programming course. NOT medical advice. Do not use for real patients.

## General danger signs (any one of these means urgent referral)
- Not able to drink or breastfeed, or vomits everything
- Convulsions (fits), now or during this illness
- Very sleepy, hard to wake, or unconscious
- Fast or difficult breathing, or chest indrawing in a child
- Heavy bleeding that does not stop with firm pressure
- Any fever in a baby younger than 2 months
- Severe dehydration (see RCC-02)

## How to refer
1. Tell the nurse in charge immediately.
2. Phone the district hospital on the referral radio, channel 4, or by mobile phone if there is a signal. Say: the patient's age, the main problem, the danger signs, and what you have already given.
3. Fill in the yellow referral form (two copies). One copy goes with the patient, one stays in the referral file.
4. While waiting for transport, keep the patient warm, keep giving ORS if they can drink, and check breathing every 15 minutes.

## Transport
The clinic's own vehicle is used first. If it is not available, the community transport list is pinned behind the reception desk. Never delay referral to wait for test results.
