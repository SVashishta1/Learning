# Vaccine fridge and cold chain (Riverside Community Clinic, fictional protocol RCC-06)

> FICTIONAL TRAINING SAMPLE. Written for a programming course. NOT medical advice. Do not use for real patients.

## Safe temperature
Vaccines must be kept between +2 °C and +8 °C at all times. Freezing damages many vaccines just as much as heat does.

## Twice-daily check
1. Read the fridge thermometer every morning when the clinic opens and every afternoon before closing.
2. Write both readings, the time and your initials on the temperature chart taped to the fridge door.
3. Never leave a gap: if a reading was missed, write "missed" and the reason.

## If the temperature is outside +2 °C to +8 °C
1. Do not use any vaccine from the fridge until the nurse in charge has decided.
2. Move the vaccines to the backup cool box with frozen-then-conditioned ice packs, and keep the box closed.
3. Write down the temperature, the time you found it, and how long it may have been outside the safe range.
4. Mark the affected vaccines "DO NOT USE: waiting for decision" and inform the district cold-chain officer.

## Power cuts
During a power cut, keep the fridge door closed. A full fridge stays cold for several hours. If the power is off for more than 4 hours, move vaccines to the cool box and record it on the temperature chart.
