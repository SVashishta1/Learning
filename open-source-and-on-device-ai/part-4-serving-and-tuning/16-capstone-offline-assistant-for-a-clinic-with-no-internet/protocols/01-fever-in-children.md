# Fever in children (Riverside Community Clinic, fictional protocol RCC-01)

> FICTIONAL TRAINING SAMPLE. Written for a programming course. NOT medical advice. Do not use for real patients.

## When to use this protocol
Use for children from 2 months to 12 years with a temperature of 38.0 °C or higher. Babies younger than 2 months with any fever go straight to the Danger signs and referral protocol (RCC-05).

## First steps
1. Measure the temperature with the clinic's digital thermometer and write it in the visit book with the time.
2. Weigh the child. Medicines in this clinic are always given by weight, using the laminated dosing chart on the wall of room 2.
3. Check for danger signs (see RCC-05): not able to drink, vomiting everything, convulsions, very sleepy or hard to wake, fast or difficult breathing.

## Care at the clinic
- Give fever medicine only as written on the dosing chart for the child's weight. Never guess a dose.
- Offer frequent small drinks. Breastfed babies should keep breastfeeding.
- Remove extra blankets and thick clothes. Do not use cold water or alcohol rubs.
- Re-check the temperature after 1 hour.

## Send home or refer?
- Send home if the child is drinking, alert, and the temperature is coming down. Tell the family to come back the same day if any danger sign appears, or after 2 days if the fever has not gone.
- Refer to the district hospital if any danger sign is present, if fever lasts more than 5 days, or if the child looks worse at the 1-hour check.
