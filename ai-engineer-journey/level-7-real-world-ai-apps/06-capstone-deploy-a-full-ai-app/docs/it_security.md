# Acme Robotics: IT Security Rules

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use the company password manager. Never reuse your work password on other websites. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the HR portal and source code repositories.

## Devices
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour at security@acme-robotics.example.

## Phishing
If you receive a suspicious email, do not click links. Use the "Report phishing" button in your email client. The security team runs a phishing simulation every quarter.

## AI tools
Employees may use approved AI assistants for work. Never paste customer personal data, passwords or unreleased source code into unapproved AI tools. The list of approved tools is on the intranet.
