# Acme Robotics: Vacation and Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Leave is earned monthly at a rate of 2.08 days per month.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used before March 31, otherwise they expire. Exceptions require written approval from the HR director.

## Requesting leave
Requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 3 days. Your manager approves or rejects requests within 5 working days.

## Sick leave
Employees receive up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within the first year.
