# SwiftHaul carrier agreement (summary)

## Service level
SwiftHaul must deliver at least 95% of Northwind parcels on time, measured per calendar month across all North region lanes.

## Service credits
For each full percentage point below 95% in a month, SwiftHaul credits Northwind 5% of that month's SwiftHaul invoice, capped at 25% of the invoice.

## Recovery plan
If on-time performance is below 90% in any month, SwiftHaul must deliver a written recovery plan within 10 working days of Northwind's request. Two consecutive months below 90% allow Northwind to move up to 50% of the volume to another carrier without penalty.

## Notice
Credits must be claimed in writing within 30 days of the month end. The September 2026 SwiftHaul invoice is £48,000.
