# Customer compensation policy (summary)

## Late Express parcels
If an Express parcel is late for a reason within Northwind's or its carriers' control, the Express surcharge (£4.50 per parcel) is refunded automatically as account credit.

## Late Standard parcels
Standard parcels 2 or more working days late: the customer may request a credit of 20% of the shipping fee.

## Proactive contact
When a known incident affects more than 50 parcels, customer service sends a proactive apology email to affected customers within 2 working days. Goodwill credits above £500 per customer need approval from the Head of Customer Service.
