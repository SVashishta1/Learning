# Customer brief: Northwind Logistics, phase 2 (fictional)

*The pilot from lessons 02 and 03 (the Shipment Reply Assistant) went live. Two months later this email arrives. As in lesson 02, the customer describes a solution; your job is to find the problem.*

**From:** Joanna Wiśniewska, Claims Team Lead (Kraków), Northwind Logistics
**Cc:** Dana Okafor (VP Customer Operations), Marek Nowak (IT Security)
**Subject:** Can the AI do claims too?

Hi team,

Dana showed us the reply assistant and the support team loves it. My team handles damage, loss and delay claims and we are buried: about 600 claims a month, 8 handlers, and a backlog of 3 weeks. Customers chase us by phone, which slows us down even more.

Could you build an AI that **approves claims automatically**? Most of them are simple. The rules are in our claims policy (v3.2) and the delivery terms (v5.0). Our handlers spend most of their time looking up shipments in the TMS and writing the same emails in English, Dutch and Polish.

Two things worry me: we had a fraud attempt last month (an email asking us to pay to a "new" bank account), and finance says nothing above EUR 1,000 goes out without a supervisor.

Could you come to Kraków next week?

Joanna
