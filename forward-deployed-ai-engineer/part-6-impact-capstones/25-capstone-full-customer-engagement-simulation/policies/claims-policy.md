# Northwind claims policy (version 3.2)
> **FICTIONAL POLICY for the course simulation.** Northwind Logistics and these rules are invented for learning.

## Liability limit
For shipments that are not insured, Northwind pays at most EUR 10 per kilogram of the gross weight of the shipment, and never more than the amount claimed or the value of the goods.

## Insured shipments
If the shipment was insured, Northwind pays the proven value of the lost or damaged goods, up to the declared value on the consignment note.

## Time limits
Visible damage must be written on the proof of delivery (POD) or reported within 7 calendar days of delivery. If the POD already notes damage, the claim may be filed within 30 days. Loss must be reported within 30 days of the expected delivery date. A shipment counts as lost when it has not been delivered 7 days after the expected delivery date.

## Evidence required
Damage claims need photos of the damaged goods and the packaging. Without photos we ask the customer for them and do not decide yet. Loss claims need the value of the goods.

## Delay compensation
For Express shipments delivered more than 48 hours late, Northwind refunds the Express shipping fee. Standard shipments have no compensation for delay.

## Approval limits
Claims handlers may approve settlements up to EUR 1,000. Settlements above EUR 1,000 must be approved by a claims supervisor. Payment details are never taken from an email: credits always go to the bank account on the customer's contract.

## Appeals
A customer can appeal a decision within 14 days by replying to the decision email. A different handler reviews the appeal.
