# Northwind delivery terms (version 5.0)
> **FICTIONAL POLICY for the course simulation.** Northwind Logistics and these rules are invented for learning.

## Proof of delivery
The receiver signs the proof of delivery (POD). "Signed clean" means the receiver saw no damage at delivery. A remark such as "damaged" or "dented" on the POD is evidence of damage at delivery.

## Express service
Express shipments are delivered within 24 hours inside the Benelux and within 48 hours elsewhere in the EU.

## Standard service
Standard shipments are delivered within 2 to 5 working days. Delivery dates are estimates.

## Packaging
The sender must pack goods so that they survive normal handling. Damage caused by poor packaging can be refused after inspection.
