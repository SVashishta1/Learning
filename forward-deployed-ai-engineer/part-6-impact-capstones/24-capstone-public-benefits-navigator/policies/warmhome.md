# WarmHome (help with heating bills)
> **FICTIONAL POLICY, FOR LEARNING ONLY.** Brightwater County and this programme do not exist. Never use this to decide real benefits.

## What it is
WarmHome pays part of your winter heating bill directly to your energy company, from November to March.

## Who can get it
You can get WarmHome if you live in Brightwater County, you pay a heating or energy bill for your home, and your household's gross monthly income is at or below 150% of the BBNA. Households with someone aged 60 or over, or a child under 6, are paid first.

## Income limit
Gross monthly income must be at or below 150% of the BBNA: 1,950 for 1 person, 2,640 for 2, 3,330 for 3, 4,020 for 4. Add 690 for each extra person.

## Documents you need
- A recent energy or heating bill in the applicant's name
- Proof of income for the last 30 days for every adult

## How to apply
Fill in form WH-1 before 31 January. Payments go straight to the energy company named on your bill.
