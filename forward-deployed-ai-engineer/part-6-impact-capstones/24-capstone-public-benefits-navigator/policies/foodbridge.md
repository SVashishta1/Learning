# FoodBridge (food help)
> **FICTIONAL POLICY, FOR LEARNING ONLY.** Brightwater County and this programme do not exist. Never use this to decide real benefits.

## What it is
FoodBridge puts money for groceries on a card every month, so families with low income can buy healthy food.

## Who can get it
You can get FoodBridge if you live in Brightwater County and your household's gross monthly income is at or below 130% of the Brightwater Basic Needs Amount (BBNA) for your household size. Everyone who lives with you and shares meals counts in the household.

## Income limit
Gross monthly income (before tax) must be at or below 130% of the BBNA: 1,690 for a household of 1, 2,288 for 2, 2,886 for 3, 3,484 for 4. Add 598 for each extra person. Count wages, pensions and benefits paid in cash.

## Documents you need
- Photo ID of the applicant
- Proof of address in Brightwater County (a bill or tenancy agreement from the last 3 months)
- Proof of income for the last 30 days for every adult (payslips, pension letter)

## How to apply
Fill in form FB-1 online, by post, or at any county service centre. A caseworker calls you within 10 working days. If you have less than 100 in cash and no income this month, ask for an emergency decision within 3 days.
