# KidCare Health (health cover for children)
> **FICTIONAL POLICY, FOR LEARNING ONLY.** Brightwater County and this programme do not exist. Never use this to decide real benefits.

## What it is
KidCare Health pays for doctor visits, medicines, dental and eye care for children.

## Who can get it
Children under 19 who live in Brightwater County can get KidCare Health if their household's gross monthly income is at or below 250% of the Brightwater Basic Needs Amount (BBNA). Adults are not covered by KidCare.

## Income limit
Gross monthly income must be at or below 250% of the BBNA: 3,250 for a household of 1, 4,400 for 2, 5,550 for 3, 6,700 for 4. Add 1,150 for each extra person.

## Documents you need
- Proof of age for each child (birth certificate or passport)
- Proof of address in Brightwater County
- Proof of income for the last 30 days for every adult

## How to apply
Fill in form KC-1. Children are covered from the first day of the month you apply, once approved.
