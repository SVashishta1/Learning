# School Meals (free school lunches)
> **FICTIONAL POLICY, FOR LEARNING ONLY.** Brightwater County and this programme do not exist. Never use this to decide real benefits.

## What it is
Children in school get a free lunch every school day.

## Who can get it
Children aged 5 to 18 who go to school in Brightwater County can get free school meals if the household's gross monthly income is at or below 185% of the BBNA, or if the household already gets FoodBridge (then no income check is needed).

## Income limit
Gross monthly income must be at or below 185% of the BBNA: 2,405 for 1 person, 3,256 for 2, 4,107 for 3, 4,958 for 4. Add 851 for each extra person.

## Documents you need
- Name of each child's school
- Proof of income for the last 30 days, unless you get FoodBridge (then your FoodBridge letter)

## How to apply
Fill in form SM-1 or tell your child's school office. One form covers all children in the household.
