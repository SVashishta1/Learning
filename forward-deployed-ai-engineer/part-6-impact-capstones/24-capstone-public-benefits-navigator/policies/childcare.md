# ChildCare Assist (help paying for childcare)
> **FICTIONAL POLICY, FOR LEARNING ONLY.** Brightwater County and this programme do not exist. Never use this to decide real benefits.

## What it is
ChildCare Assist pays most of the cost of a registered childminder or nursery while parents work or study.

## Who can get it
You can get ChildCare Assist for children under 13 if every adult in the household works or studies, and the household's gross monthly income is at or below 200% of the BBNA.

## Income limit
Gross monthly income must be at or below 200% of the BBNA: 2,600 for 1 person, 3,520 for 2, 4,440 for 3, 5,360 for 4. Add 920 for each extra person.

## Documents you need
- Proof of age for each child (birth certificate or passport)
- Proof of work or study for every adult (contract, timetable or letter)
- Proof of income for the last 30 days for every adult

## How to apply
Fill in form CC-1. You choose a registered provider; the county pays them directly.
