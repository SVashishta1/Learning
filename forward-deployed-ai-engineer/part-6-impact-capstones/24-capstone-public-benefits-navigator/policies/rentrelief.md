# RentRelief (help with rent)
> **FICTIONAL POLICY, FOR LEARNING ONLY.** Brightwater County and this programme do not exist. Never use this to decide real benefits.

## What it is
RentRelief pays up to 3 months of rent to your landlord when rent takes too big a share of your income.

## Who can get it
You can get RentRelief if you rent your home in Brightwater County, your monthly rent is more than 40% of your gross monthly income, and your household's gross monthly income is at or below 200% of the BBNA. Households with an eviction notice are handled first.

## Income limit
Gross monthly income must be at or below 200% of the BBNA: 2,600 for 1 person, 3,520 for 2, 4,440 for 3, 5,360 for 4. Add 920 for each extra person.

## Documents you need
- Tenancy agreement (lease) with the monthly rent
- Proof of income for the last 30 days for every adult
- Eviction notice, if you have one

## How to apply
Fill in form RR-1. The county pays the landlord directly; the landlord must agree to receive the payment.
