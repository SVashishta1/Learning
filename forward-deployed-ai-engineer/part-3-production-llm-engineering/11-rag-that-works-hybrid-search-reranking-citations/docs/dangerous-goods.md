# Dangerous goods

## What we never carry
Northwind does not carry fireworks, flammable gases, explosives, radioactive material, or infectious substances, on any service.

## Lithium batteries
Lithium-ion batteries packed with equipment or contained in equipment (UN3481) are accepted on road services when the parcel carries the lithium battery mark. Standalone lithium-ion batteries shipped on their own (UN3480) are not accepted on any service, because our network includes air sectors to the islands.

## Aerosols and limited quantities
Aerosols such as deodorants and spray paints are accepted as limited quantities by road, up to 1 litre per item and 30 kilograms per parcel, with the limited quantity mark.

## Paperwork
Every dangerous goods shipment needs a dangerous goods declaration on form DG-2, completed by the sender before collection. Drivers will refuse a parcel without a declaration.
