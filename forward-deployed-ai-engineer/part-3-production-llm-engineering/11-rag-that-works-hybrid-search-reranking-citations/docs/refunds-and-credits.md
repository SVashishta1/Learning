# Refunds and service credits

## Late Express parcels
If an Express parcel is delivered late for a reason within our control, the Express surcharge is refunded automatically as account credit. No form is needed; the credit appears on the next invoice.

## Late Standard parcels
If a Standard parcel arrives 2 or more working days late, the customer can request a credit of 20% of the shipping fee for that parcel. Requests must be made within 30 days of the delivery date.

## Using credits
Service credits are applied to future invoices and expire after 12 months. Credits cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Duplicate charges and billing disputes
Duplicate card charges are refunded to the original card within 5 working days of being reported. Any other billing dispute must be raised within 60 days of the invoice date by emailing the accounts team with the invoice number.
