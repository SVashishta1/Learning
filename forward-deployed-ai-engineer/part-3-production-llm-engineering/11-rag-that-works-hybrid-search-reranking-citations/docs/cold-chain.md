# Cold chain service

## Temperature ranges
Our cold chain service has two ranges: Chilled keeps goods between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius, and Frozen keeps goods at minus 18 degrees Celsius or colder. Chilled parcels must reach the customer within 24 hours of collection.

## Monitoring and excursions
Every cold chain vehicle and cold room carries a temperature logger that records every 5 minutes. If the temperature leaves the agreed range for more than 30 minutes, this is called an excursion, and we inform the customer within 2 hours with the logger data.

## Pharmaceutical shipments
Pharmaceutical shipments follow Good Distribution Practice (GDP). Medicines are only handled at our cold hubs in Leeds and Bristol, which are licensed for this work.

## Restrictions
Cold chain parcels cannot be redirected to a new address after collection, and they cannot use Saturday delivery.
