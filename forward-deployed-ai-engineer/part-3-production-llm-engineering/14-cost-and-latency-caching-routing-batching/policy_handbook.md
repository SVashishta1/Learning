# Northwind Logistics customer policy handbook

## Delivery promise and service levels

### Services and delivery times
Northwind Logistics offers three services. Express is delivered the next working day by 17:00 when the parcel is collected before the 15:00 cut-off. Standard is delivered within 3 working days. Economy is delivered within 5 working days and is meant for non-urgent stock moves.

### What counts as a working day
A working day is Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays. Parcels collected on a Friday after the cut-off are treated as collected on Monday. Deliveries to the Scottish Highlands, the Northern Isles and the Isle of Man take one extra working day on every service.

### Saturday delivery
Saturday delivery is available for Express only, as an add-on costing £9.50 per parcel. It must be booked before the Friday 15:00 cut-off. Saturday delivery is not available to the Scottish Highlands or the islands.

### Our on-time target
Northwind's on-time target is 95% of parcels delivered within the promised time, measured every calendar month per service. Monthly on-time results for each customer account are shown in the customer portal under Reports.

## Claims for damaged and lost shipments

### Reporting damage
Visible damage must be noted on the proof of delivery or reported within 48 hours of delivery. Hidden damage, found only after unpacking, must be reported within 7 days of delivery. Keep the original packaging for 14 days, because our claims team may ask to inspect it.

### How to make a claim
Claims are made with form CL-7 in the customer portal. Attach photos of the outer box, the inner packaging and the damaged items, plus the commercial invoice showing the value of the goods. A claim without photos cannot be assessed.

### When a shipment is treated as lost
A shipment is declared lost when it has had no tracking scan for 10 working days. Before that point our investigations team searches the hubs, so customers should report a missing parcel but wait for the investigation result before claiming.

### How much we pay
Our standard liability is limited to £50 per parcel. Customers who bought Enhanced Cover are covered up to £2,500 per parcel. Approved claims are paid within 30 days, either as account credit or by bank transfer.

## Refunds and service credits

### Late Express parcels
If an Express parcel is delivered late for a reason within our control, the Express surcharge is refunded automatically as account credit. No form is needed; the credit appears on the next invoice.

### Late Standard parcels
If a Standard parcel arrives 2 or more working days late, the customer can request a credit of 20% of the shipping fee for that parcel. Requests must be made within 30 days of the delivery date.

### Using credits
Service credits are applied to future invoices and expire after 12 months. Credits cannot be exchanged for cash.

### Duplicate charges and billing disputes
Duplicate card charges are refunded to the original card within 5 working days of being reported. Any other billing dispute must be raised within 60 days of the invoice date by emailing the accounts team with the invoice number.

## Dangerous goods

### What we never carry
Northwind does not carry fireworks, flammable gases, explosives, radioactive material, or infectious substances, on any service.

### Lithium batteries
Lithium-ion batteries packed with equipment or contained in equipment (UN3481) are accepted on road services when the parcel carries the lithium battery mark. Standalone lithium-ion batteries shipped on their own (UN3480) are not accepted on any service, because our network includes air sectors to the islands.

### Aerosols and limited quantities
Aerosols such as deodorants and spray paints are accepted as limited quantities by road, up to 1 litre per item and 30 kilograms per parcel, with the limited quantity mark.

### Paperwork
Every dangerous goods shipment needs a dangerous goods declaration on form DG-2, completed by the sender before collection. Drivers will refuse a parcel without a declaration.

## Cold chain service

### Temperature ranges
Our cold chain service has two ranges: Chilled keeps goods between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius, and Frozen keeps goods at minus 18 degrees Celsius or colder. Chilled parcels must reach the customer within 24 hours of collection.

### Monitoring and excursions
Every cold chain vehicle and cold room carries a temperature logger that records every 5 minutes. If the temperature leaves the agreed range for more than 30 minutes, this is called an excursion, and we inform the customer within 2 hours with the logger data.

### Pharmaceutical shipments
Pharmaceutical shipments follow Good Distribution Practice (GDP). Medicines are only handled at our cold hubs in Leeds and Bristol, which are licensed for this work.

### Restrictions
Cold chain parcels cannot be redirected to a new address after collection, and they cannot use Saturday delivery.

## Address changes and collections

### Changing a delivery address
The delivery address can be changed free of charge in the customer portal until the parcel is dispatched from the hub. After dispatch, a redirection costs £6 and adds one working day to the delivery time. Cold chain and dangerous goods parcels cannot be redirected.

### Booking a collection
Collections must be booked by 14:00 on the working day before the collection. Regular daily collections can be set up by the account manager.

### Saturday collections
Saturday collections are available only inside the M25, Greater Manchester and Leeds, for an extra £12 per collection.

### Failed collections
If the driver arrives and the goods are not ready, the collection is marked as failed and a £15 fee is charged. A new collection must be booked.
