# Training session plan: Northwind Shipment Reply Assistant (filled example)

**Audience:** 10 support agents (Rotterdam pilot group) + Priya (team lead) · **Length:** 75 minutes, in person, laptops open
**Trainers:** FDE engineer + Sam Lee (senior agent, pilot champion) · **Date:** 2027-01-12 (week before go-live)
**Goal:** after the session every agent can draft, check, edit and send a reply with the assistant, knows when NOT to trust it, and knows how to report a bad draft.

| Time | Part | What happens | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-10 min | Why and what | Priya: why we do this (19 h first-response time, customers waiting). FDE: what the assistant does and does not do (drafts only, agent sends, no bank details). | 3 slides |
| 10-20 min | Live demo | Sam drafts 3 real (anonymised) tickets: a simple "where is my shipment", a delay with the delivery-terms policy, and a case where the assistant says it doesn't know. | test HelpDesk |
| 20-45 min | Hands-on, in pairs | Each pair works 6 practice tickets on the test system. Checklist per draft: tracking number right? status right? promise matches policy? tone OK? | practice ticket list, checklist card |
| 45-55 min | When not to trust it | Show 3 deliberately wrong drafts (old status, invented compensation, wrong customer name). Agents find the error. Rule: **you** are responsible for what you send. | 3 bad drafts |
| 55-65 min | Reporting problems | Use the "Bad draft" button / ticket category; what to write (ticket id, what was wrong). Who reads it (Priya + IT) and how fast. | screenshot handout |
| 65-75 min | Questions and quiz | 5-question quiz (below); open questions; fears (job security) answered honestly by Priya. | quiz |

**Quiz (answers in the trainer notes)**
1. The draft promises a refund. What do you check before sending? (the delivery-terms policy section it cites)
2. The assistant says "I could not find this shipment". What now? (check the tracking number with the customer; do not guess)
3. Can the assistant send emails by itself? (no)
4. Where do you report a wrong draft? (Bad draft button)
5. A customer asks if an AI wrote the email. What do we say? (the agreed wording from Priya)

**After the session**
- Day 1 and 3 of go-live: Sam and the FDE engineer sit with the agents (floor walking), 2 hours each.
- Week 2: 15-minute check-in: top 3 problems, fixes announced.
- Materials (slides, checklist card, recording) in the support wiki; new hires get a 20-minute version.
