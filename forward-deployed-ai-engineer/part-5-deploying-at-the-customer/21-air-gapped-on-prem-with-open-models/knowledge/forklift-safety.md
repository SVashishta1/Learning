# Forklift safety (Northwind warehouse procedure WH-03, fictional)

## Daily check
Before the first use each shift, check brakes, horn, lights, forks, chains and tyres, and record the check on the tablet. A truck that fails the check is tagged "do not use" and reported to maintenance.

## Speed and pedestrians
Maximum speed inside the warehouse is 10 km/h, and 5 km/h in aisles with pedestrians. Sound the horn at every blind corner. Pedestrians must stay in the green walkways.

## Battery charging
Charge batteries only in the charging room, with the ventilation running. No open flames or smoking within 5 metres.
