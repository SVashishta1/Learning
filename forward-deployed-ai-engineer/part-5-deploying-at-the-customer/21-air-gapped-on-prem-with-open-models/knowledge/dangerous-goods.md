# Dangerous goods (Northwind warehouse procedure WH-12, fictional)

## Accepting dangerous goods
Only staff with valid ADR training may accept dangerous goods. Check that every package has the correct UN number and hazard label, and that the transport document lists the same UN numbers.

## Leaking package
Stop work in the area and keep people at least 10 metres away. Do not touch the leak. Call the shift lead and the site emergency number 2222. Use the spill kit only if you are trained and the safety data sheet allows it.

## Storage
Store flammable liquids (class 3) only in cage C. Never store oxidisers (class 5.1) next to flammable liquids.
