# Cold-chain shipments (Northwind warehouse procedure WH-07, fictional)

## Temperature limits
Chilled goods must stay between 2 and 8 °C. Frozen goods must stay at -18 °C or colder. Check the logger display when the pallet arrives and write the reading on the receiving form.

## Logger alarm on arrival
If the temperature logger shows an alarm, do not unload into general storage. Move the pallet to quarantine bay Q2, photograph the logger display, and call the shift lead. The customer decides whether the goods are released or destroyed.

## Reefer trailer failure
If a reefer trailer's cooling unit fails in the yard, plug it into shore power at dock 14 or 15 and call maintenance. Goods may stay in a failed trailer for at most 2 hours before they must be moved to the cold room.
