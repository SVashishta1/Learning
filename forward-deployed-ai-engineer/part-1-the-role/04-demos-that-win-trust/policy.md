# Northwind delivery terms (excerpt)

[DT-3.1] If a shipment is delayed, Northwind informs the customer of the new expected delivery time as soon as it is known.
[DT-3.2] For delays over 24 hours caused by Northwind, business customers may request a 10% credit on the transport charge.
[DT-4.1] Proof of delivery (name of the person who signed, time and place) is available on request within 2 working days.
[DT-5.1] Damage must be reported within 7 days of delivery. Damage claims are handled by the Claims team, not Customer Support.
