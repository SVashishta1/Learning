# Discovery question bank

Pick the questions that fit. You will never ask all of them in one meeting. The ⭐ ones are the questions to ask in almost every first meeting. Each group matches a section of `notes_template.md`.

## The problem
- ⭐ In your own words, what problem are we solving?
- ⭐ Why now? What changed?
- What happens if nothing changes for a year?
- What have you already tried? Why didn't it work?
- If you had a magic wand, what would be different tomorrow morning?
- Is this one problem or several? Which hurts most?

## Current process
- ⭐ Walk me through the last time this happened, step by step.
- ⭐ Can you show me on your screen?
- What triggers the process? What does "done" look like?
- Which step takes longest? Which step goes wrong most?
- Where do you look things up when you are unsure?
- Are there workarounds, spreadsheets or personal notes people rely on?

## Pain and impact
- ⭐ How long does one of these take? How many per week?
- What does a mistake cost (money, time, an angry customer)?
- How does this show up in your metrics (response time, backlog, churn, overtime)?
- Who complains about it: customers, staff, management?
- Where does that number come from? Can we see the report?

## Users and volume
- ⭐ Who would use the solution day to day? How many people?
- How comfortable are they with new tools?
- What languages do they work in?
- What are the peaks (time of day, season, events)?
- Will they use it on a desktop, a phone, in a browser, inside an existing tool?

## Data
- ⭐ Where does the information for this live?
- What format is it in (database, PDF, Excel, email, scanned paper)?
- How much is there? How old? How often does it change?
- How clean is it? Can you show me a messy example?
- Who owns it, and who can give us access?
- Is there labelled history (past answers, decisions) we could test against?

## Systems and integrations
- ⭐ Which systems are involved? Is there an API, a database, or only a screen?
- How do people log in (SSO? which identity provider)?
- Are there firewalls, VPNs or private networks between us and the data?
- Can we get a test (sandbox) environment?
- Who maintains each system, and how fast do they respond to requests?

## Security, privacy and compliance
- ⭐ What data is sensitive (personal, financial, health, secret)?
- ⭐ Where may data be processed? Must it stay in a country, a cloud, your building?
- Which regulations or contracts apply (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, customer contracts)?
- Who must approve a new tool or data flow? How long does the review take?
- May an AI act on its own, or must a human approve each action?
- What must be logged or auditable?

## Success
- ⭐ How will you judge, in 3 months, whether this worked?
- ⭐ What is the number today (the baseline)?
- What would be "good enough" for a pilot? What would be "amazing"?
- What would make you stop the project?
- Who signs off that it worked?

## Constraints
- Deadline? What is driving it?
- Budget for the pilot? For production?
- Who on your side has time to help, and how much?
- Change freezes, holidays, audits coming up?

## Stakeholders
- ⭐ Who decides? Who pays?
- Who could block this? What would worry them?
- Who will be most affected? Have they been told?
- Who are the champions we should involve early?

## Closing
- ⭐ Did I get this right? (read back the problem and success measure)
- ⭐ What should I have asked that I didn't?
- Who else should we talk to?
- What is the best way to reach you with follow-up questions?
