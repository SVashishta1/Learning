**Subject:** AI chatbot for our support team?

- Our policies (claims, refunds, delivery terms) are PDFs on SharePoint. Rate cards are in Excel.

Could your team come on site for a discovery workshop? We'd love a demo of what's possible within a quarter.

Dana
