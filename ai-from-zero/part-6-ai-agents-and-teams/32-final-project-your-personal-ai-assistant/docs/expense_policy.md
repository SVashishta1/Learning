# Acme Robotics: Travel and Expense Policy

## Meals
While travelling, meals are reimbursed up to 60 euros per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Client dinners require pre-approval from a director and are capped at 90 euros per person.

## Flights and trains
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights over 6 hours. Trains are preferred for journeys under 4 hours.

## Hotels
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 180 euros per night in major cities (London, Paris, New York, Tokyo) and 130 euros per night elsewhere.

## Submitting expenses
Submit receipts in the expense app within 30 days of the purchase. Expenses submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Home office
Each employee can claim a one-time home office allowance of 500 euros for equipment such as a desk, chair or monitor.
