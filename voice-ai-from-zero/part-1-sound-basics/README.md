# Part 1 · Sound basics

To a computer, sound is just **a long list of numbers**: how far the microphone was pushed, measured thousands of times per second. Every voice AI starts by getting those numbers.

| # | Project | What you'll have |
|---|---------|------------------|
| 01 | [Record and play your voice](01-record-and-play-your-voice) | a 5-second voice recorder in Python |
| 02 | [See your voice](02-see-your-voice) | pictures of your voice: the wave and its "fingerprint" |
| 03 | [Voice recorder in the browser](03-voice-recorder-in-the-browser) | record, play back and watch a live volume meter, with no install |

Install once: `pip install sounddevice soundfile numpy matplotlib`
